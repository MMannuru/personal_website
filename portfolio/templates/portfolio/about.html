A Fascination with Dumb Machines 💻

In recent decades, we’ve achieved some truly mind-blowing things with machines. We’ve built computers that can predict diseases, simulate entire ecosystems, and even drive cars. Yet, these incredibly powerful machines are so unbelievably dumb. They can only do exactly what they’re told. They follow the simplest of instructions at insane speeds and flip billions of transistors on and off like tiny switches.

What’s amazing, though, is that we’ve figured out how to teach these machines to learn. It’s literally like a little child. At first, they start knowing nothing—but the more they see, the more connections they make, and the better they get at recognizing patterns. They can’t truly think like humans, but they can mimic the human learning process by absorbing vast amounts of information and adjusting their behavior based on feedback.

And that’s just one example. We’ve also created machines that communicate across the globe in milliseconds, sending data back and forth to keep the entire world connected in real-time. It’s crazy to think that we’ve taken raw, dumb hardware and turned it into a tool that can revolutionize so many parts of human life. I’m just starting my journey, so I’m still figuring out my exact interests. But I’m eager to explore our digital world at its core 🌍.
